# Process editor

## Intent

Very different people make the app's processes (processes) their own (issue #40): "Don't ask me before the merge", "the tests are `npm test` here", "research → draft → my edit". They should read a process as a recipe, change it in a form or in words, build their own from ready steps, try it without consequences, and run it from a folder's buttons and from automations, never touching code unless a step is missing. The proposal and its plan: the note "Редактор и каталог процессов".

## Spec

- **Words.** The user sees "process" and "step"; never a node, an edge, a key or a step id (those are under "More" in a step's card). Steps and processes are named in the user's language through the plugins' strings: `process:<id>`, `step:<id>`, `outcome:<key>:<value>`, `question:<step>:<id>`, `option:<step>:<id>`, `field:<step>:<id>`, `param:<step>:<key>`, `key:<key>`.
- **Catalog** (`Processes` above the tree, next to Automations, `openPage("process-editor", "catalog")`): a card per process with what it does, its step count, how often it asks you, where it is used (folders' buttons, automations, other processes, tasks now) and why it cannot run with a "Fix" button; search by name and by step; filters All · Mine · From plugins · With problems; templates on the side.
- **Recipe** (`openPage("process-editor", "process", { id, node?, taskId? })`): numbered steps grouped by the task's status, who does each (you, an agent, the app), where it goes when not simply on ("passed → 8 · otherwise ↩ 5"), the tasks on each step now; a step's card on the right (a full screen on a phone) with what it asks, its settings (a setting's value shown as such), its status and where it goes next, and under "More" what it gets and gives.
- **Change** (`E`, or "Change"): the same page; a step's card becomes a form: its name in the recipe, its settings (a setting's value is the hint, typing sets it for this process only, the arrow brings the setting back), the task's status, where each answer or result leads, "Skip this step". Changes go to a draft kept on the server (it outlives the page) with "Draft · N changes · Undo · Discard all · Apply" (`Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Enter`); changed steps are marked "~", added "+". "Apply" says where the process is used and that running tasks finish on their version, and asks what changed for the history.
- **What to change?** A field above the recipe (`/` puts the cursor there; "Change this step in words" in the Process tab of a task's details fills in the step). An agent gets the process as JSON with the steps it may use and answers with the changed process, a question, or "a step is missing". Its proposal is checked; new problems go back to it, twice at most. The user sees the proposal as one recipe with +, ~ and struck-through steps and the agent's note on how it understood the request: "Take into the draft" (then Apply as any change), more words in the field to refine it, or Cancel. "A step is missing" offers "Ask an agent to make the step" (a customization of the app, through customize-app).
- **Any agent**: the agent is the setting "Agent for changing processes", else the folder's. One that cannot answer without a terminal runs in one nobody sees and talks with `pitwall answer|ask|progress` (`ctx.agents.run(..., { fallback: "session" })`); "Show the terminal" shows it, a permission it waits for is shown with "Open the terminal". After `silenceSeconds` (60) of silence: "Wait 5 more minutes · Stop"; after `answerMinutes` (5) it is stopped, with "What it did". Once, after such a run, the field offers an installed agent that answers without a terminal for changing processes ("Not now": a week; "Do not offer again").
- **Build your own**: "New" in the catalog opens one window with templates (their copy in the user's language), "describe it in words" (an empty process whose agent starts at once) and "Start from an empty process". In the edit mode a "+" between two steps (on hover; Insert on the step picked) and "Add a step" at the end open the step picker: search by what a step does, "Often", groups by who does it (you, agent, checks and commands, git, GitHub), processes as one step; a step that needs what no step makes yet is greyed with why, or comes with the step that makes it. A step's card moves it (Alt+↑/↓), duplicates it, removes it (Del, with Undo); "Go different ways by…" makes a branch on its answers or results, "One way on" takes it back. A problem "a step needs what nothing makes" has "Add <the step that makes it> before it". An empty process shows the first steps people add.
- **A process inside a process**: any process can be a step ("Processes" in the step picker, with its step count). In the recipe it is one row, "N steps ▸" unfolds its steps in place ("2.1", read only), and under it what it is given when that is not the same key ("description = the agent's answer"). Its card: a link to it, "What it gets" (in the edit mode a pick of what this process makes, or "nothing: it asks for it itself"), what it gives back, and "Always the current one / Its own copy" (`pin`). The summary counts its questions ("asks you 3 times (2 inside “Development”)"); a process used inside others says so on its page ("What you change here reaches them too"). Shift+click picks steps that follow each other; "Take them out into a process" makes them a process of the user's own, run in their place (refused, with why, when something leads into their middle or they lead out to different places). On a task, the line over its name shows the step it is on; the Process tab of its details shows the steps it ran.
- **Trial run** (header): a task of the process, or of its draft, in a folder whose agent and settings it uses, that stays out of the tree, the folder's tables and search; "Without consequences" skips the steps that change the world outside, "Step by step" asks before each step. The "Trials" tab lists them with the task's own panel and what each step got and gave; they are deleted after a day unless kept.
- **Run it**: under "Where it is used", "Add a button to a folder…" (it says when the folder's buttons are full), "Create a task…" and "Run it automatically…" (pick the folder, then the automation's dialog with the process picked: what to give the process from its first form, with fields of the last webhook; "When the process asks you", each question with Wait for me, an answer, an answer only "if <a step>: <its result>", or Stop the task; "What you will find" in one sentence; a question added to the process later is marked new on the automation's page until the user decides); the same in a card's menu with "Make a copy" and, for the user's own, "Delete" (not while it is used).
- **New steps**: "New step in words" (in the step picker, the steps tab, or when the agent says a step is missing) asks for its name, what the agent should do (with the task's values and other steps' results to insert), whether the agent works in the task's terminal until it is done or quickly with one answer, and what it gives back (its answer, or a pick that branches the process); "Try it on a task" shows the answer first (prompt-steps keeps it as a file). "Ask an agent to make the step" starts a customization of the app for a step with code; from the step picker a stand-in holds its place in the process until it is ready. The "Steps" tab of the catalog lists every step by who does it, with what it gets and gives and which processes use it; the user's own steps in words are changed and deleted there (not while used), a step with code is changed by asking an agent.
- **Schema** (a tab, on a wide screen only): the same process as a graph laid out by itself, top to bottom as the recipe; the next step straight below, a way that skips steps on the right, a way back on the left in amber, a way back to itself as a loop; the answers on the ways; the steps that wait for you outlined, each with its status's color. A click opens the step's card in the recipe.
- **Habits**: under a question answered the same way `habitAnswers` (5) times in a row: "You keep answering “Merge”. Not ask any more? Yes, change the process · No, keep asking"; "Yes" opens "What to change?" with the request written (nothing changes until the user takes it), "No" never offers it for that question again.
- **The original changed**: a user's version whose plugin's process got a new version says "the original changed" (its card, its page); it shows what changed in the original since, and "Take it into my version" asks an agent to do it in words; taking it records the original's new version as the base.
- **Offer to the shared repository…** (the page's "…", for the user's own processes and versions): after the user says what it is good for, an issue in the app's repository with the process as a file (`gh issue create`), rule 8.
- **Your own version**: a plugin's process is never rewritten. Applying a change writes `plugins/my-processes/processes/<plugin>-<id>.json` with `overrides`, which runs in its place: "changed by you", "Compare with the original" (one recipe with +, ~ and struck-through steps) and "Bring the original back".
- **History**: every applied version with its date and note; compare one with now, or bring it back as a draft.
- **On a task** (issue #51): the Process tab of the task's details (the core's history of its steps) ends with "Рецепт процесса" (the recipe at the task's step), "Изменить этот шаг словами" and "Настроить шаг" (slot `task.details.process`). "Why is the task here?" in the palette opens that tab. When the process changed after the task started, "process changed" in the task's header (slot `process.bar`) shows what changed and moves the task to the new version if its step is still there.

## Check

`pnpm test` runs `tests/model.test.ts`: the recipe of the development process (numbers, status groups, the ways on with their words, who does each step) and where a process is used; `tests/editor.test.ts`: drafts, applying a change as the user's own version (the plugin's file untouched), bringing the original back, moving a task to the new version; `tests/changes.test.ts`: a proposal from an agent without a terminal, one from a terminal nobody sees after its first answer was not JSON, the agent's question answered; `tests/build.test.ts`: insert, remove, move, duplicate, branch, a template's copy in Russian, taking steps out into a process; the editor's server creates processes and trials and forgets old trials; `tests/polish.test.ts`: the schema's layout of the development process, habits counted and offered, the issue that offers a process, "the original changed". By hand: open Processes above the tree, find "Development" by typing "тест", open it, click step 7; open a task that follows it, press Alt+Shift+P (its details on Process), then "Рецепт процесса". Press E on "Development", click "Tests", type another command, Ctrl+Enter, Apply: the card says "changed by you", "Compare with the original" shows the change.
